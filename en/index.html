FAQ

Common questions

I've never done anything online. Where do I start?

With the free conversation. We understand your business and suggest the first step that makes sense, which is often a well-made Instagram or a simple website.

Do I need to understand technology?

No. We handle the technical side and explain everything in plain language. You only need to know your business.

How long does a project take?

It depends on what you need: a simple page is much faster than a custom system. The timeline is set in the proposal and written into the contract before we start.

Do I need a logo to start?

No. We can also create your visual identity, and it can be done together with the website.

Do you work outside São Paulo?

Yes. In São Paulo we can meet in person, and everywhere else in Brazil everything works online.

How much does a website for a small business cost?

It depends on what your business needs: a single page costs much less than a multi-page site or a system. After the free conversation, you receive the fixed price in writing in the proposal, with no surprises along the way.

Will my website show up on Google?

The site is delivered following Google's own guidelines: correct title and description, verified in Search Console, and a sitemap submitted. Your ranking depends on competition and takes a few weeks to show up, but the site is ready to be found from day one.

How does payment work?

Via Pix (Brazil's instant bank transfer), as agreed in the contract.
